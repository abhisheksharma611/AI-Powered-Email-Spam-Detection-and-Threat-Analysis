AI-Powered Email Spam Detection — System Modules

Gmail read-only • classify → score → display • local SQLite

1. Data Acquisition & Authentication

app.py · config.py · utils/auth.py · gmail_client.py
  • Logs in with Google OAuth and fetches latest 50 Gmail IDs, batch ×20 with retry.
  • Controls sync + background scans through one pipeline, batch 10.

2. Text Preprocessing & Feature Engineering

gmail_client.py · preprocessing.py
  • Parses MIME/base64 headers and cleans HTML into plain lowercase text.
  • Extracts 6 numeric signals (length, caps, URL/phone, urgency).

3. Hybrid Classification & Risk Assessment

predictor.py · roberta_model.py · helpers.py
  • Classifies with 0.90 RoBERTa + 0.10 ensemble into 6 categories.
  • Scores urgency + sender history + keywords into 0–100 risk + summary.

4. Storage & Visualization

email_model.py · templates/ · static/ · ai_explanation.py
  • Stores results per-user in SQLite (Email, reputation, keywords).
  • Displays 12 pages: results table, analytics charts, threat console.
6 classes: spam / legitimate / promotion / phishing / malware / newsletter    Risk: Low 0–40 · Med 41–60 · High 61–100
