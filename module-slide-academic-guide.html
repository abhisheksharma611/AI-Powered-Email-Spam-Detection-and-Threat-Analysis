AI-Powered Email Spam Detection — System Modules

Gmail read-only • classify → score → display • local SQLite

1. Data Acquisition & Authentication

app.py · config.py · utils/auth.py · gmail_client.py
  • Signs in with Google (read-only) and downloads your latest 50 mails, 20 at a time.
  • Runs the same checking pipeline for quick scans and background scans.

2. Text Preprocessing & Feature Engineering

gmail_client.py · preprocessing.py
  • Opens each mail, decodes it, removes formatting → plain text.
  • Measures 6 spam clues: length, capitals, links, phone, urgency words.

3. Hybrid Classification & Risk Assessment

predictor.py · roberta_model.py · helpers.py
  • AI reads the text and votes (90% RoBERTa + 10% backup) → 1 of 6 categories.
  • Adds urgency + sender past + learnt keywords → risk 0–100 with a reason.

4. Storage & Visualization

email_model.py · templates/ · static/ · ai_explanation.py
  • Saves everything per user in a local database (4 tables).
  • Shows results table, charts and threat page — 12 pages total.
6 classes: spam / legitimate / promotion / phishing / malware / newsletter    Risk: Low 0–40 · Med 41–60 · High 61–100

What to say (60 seconds, plain words)

  1. Box 1: "The app logs in with your Google account — read-only, so it can never send or delete mail — and downloads the latest 50 mails."
  2. Box 2: "Each mail is opened, decoded and cleaned into plain text, then we measure 6 spam clues like links, capitals and urgency words."
  3. Box 3: "Two AI models vote on the category, then urgency, sender history and learnt keywords adjust it into a 0–100 risk with a written reason."
  4. Box 4: "Everything is saved per user in a local database and shown as a results table, charts and a threat page."

If the guide asks… (one-line answers)

  • Why two models? RoBERTa understands meaning; the small ensemble is a cheap backup. 90/10 blend; either can work alone.
  • Why read-only Gmail? Safety + privacy — the app physically cannot send, delete or relabel.
  • Which are threats? Only spam, phishing, malware. Promotion and newsletter are never counted as threats.
  • How is 0–100 made? Base per category × confidence, plus urgency / sender / keyword boosts, capped at 100.
  • Models missing? API returns 503 — it never invents a fake label.
  • Limits? ~200–500 ms per mail on CPU, 7-day trends, single worker (rate limits are in-process).
